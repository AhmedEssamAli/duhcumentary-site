Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers the Duhcumentary publishing tool ("the tool"), which uploads and schedules videos to accounts owned by Duhcumentary.

What the tool accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and update video details on the Duhcumentary channel (YouTube Data API).
  • Meta: permission to publish videos and Reels to the Duhcumentary Facebook Page and Instagram account (Graph API).
  • TikTok: permission to read basic profile info and post videos to the Duhcumentary TikTok account (Content Posting API).

What it stores

Only the access tokens for these owned accounts. They are kept on the owner's own computer (or in encrypted secret storage when an automated runner is used) and are never shared. The tool does not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other person.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos go only to the platforms listed above.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, in Facebook Settings > Business integrations, or in TikTok Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Revoking deletes the tool's ability to post; stored tokens are then deleted.
